Documentation

Understand your audio.

From simple explanations to detailed methods: discover what each analysis observes, how to read its results and where its limits lie.

A result is a clue, not a quality grade.

Clean means no detector raised a finding. A dash means a measurement is unavailable. Audition flagged passages and read the limits before drawing a conclusion.

Stereo & phase

  • Dual mono↗

    Identical channels or a negligible stereo difference.

  • Global polarity↗

    Left/right correlation and strong whole-track opposition.

  • Local & band phase↗

    Brief or hidden opposition across four frequency bands.

  • Stereo balance↗

    RMS level difference between left and right channels.

  • HF Stereo↗

    Stereo narrowing in the high band. Experimental cue.

Levels & dynamics

  • DC offset↗

    Mean displacement from zero, measured per channel.

  • Clipping↗

    Runs of samples at or very near full scale.

  • True peak↗

    Estimated inter-sample peaks, in dBTP.

  • Dynamics (DR)↗

    Gap between sample peak and loud-passage RMS.

  • Loudness Range (LRA)↗
