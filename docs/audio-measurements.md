This page is retained for existing links. The individual measurement pages are now separate so that method, interpretation, limits and fixtures stay next to the result they describe.

- [Integrated loudness](integrated-loudness.md)
- [Maximum momentary loudness](momentary-loudness.md)
- [Maximum short-term loudness](short-term-loudness.md)
- [Loudness range](loudness-range.md)

These measurements are computed during the decoded audio pass. Polarity and balance require exactly two decoded channels. DC offset accepts 1–32 channels. Integrated loudness, momentary/short-term maxima and LRA currently accept mono or stereo at 8–768 kHz. A saved report from an older version remains readable, but it must be analyzed again to contain measurements added after it was created.
