1//! DSD (Direct Stream Digital) support.
2//!
3//! Two genuinely separate jobs, one per file:
4//!
5//! * [`container`] — **exact** parsing of DSF (Sony) and DFF (Philips DSDIFF)
6//!   headers: magic bytes, sample rate, channel count, sample count, DST
7//!   compression flag. Byte-level format work, testable against a handcrafted
8//!   header, with no notion of audio content.
9//! * [`spectral`] — **calibrated heuristics** over an already-decoded spectrum:
10//!   spotting DSD that was converted from a PCM source ("fake DSD"), and the
11//!   mirror case of hi-res PCM that came from a DSD master. Signal processing
12//!   with measured thresholds, no notion of file layout.
13//!
14//! They were one 400-line file until the two halves were told apart; nothing
15//! in the first half calls anything in the second. Both are re-exported here,
16//! so callers keep writing `core::dsd::parse` / `core::dsd::DsdInfo`.
17
18pub mod container;
19pub mod spectral;
20
21pub use container::{parse, DsdInfo, DSD64_RATE};
22pub use spectral::{dsd_heritage_check, pcm_source_check, PcmSourceCheck, PCM_CLIFF_DB};
