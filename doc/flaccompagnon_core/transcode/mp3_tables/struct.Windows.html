Expand description

The four window shapes Layer III applies to a subband’s 36 samples.

Same idea as AAC’s, at a twelfth of the size and one stage further down the chain: these are applied inside each of the 32 subbands, not to the PCM. start and stop are the transition shapes bridging long and short blocks; both must be tested, because a frame the encoder coded as a transition carries its lattice under that shape and no other.

Fields§

§long: Vec<f64>

Plain long window, 36 samples.

§start: Vec<f64>

Long rising edge, short falling edge.

§stop: Vec<f64>

Short rising edge, long falling edge.

§short: Vec<f64>

Applied to each of the three short transforms, 12 samples.
